## 3. Curation Judgement & Notes

### The Rejection Note
![Rejected Texture](images/rejected_hero_texture.jpg)
**Why I rejected this generated image:** 
While the AI produced an impressive and highly detailed 3D geometric render, I rejected it because it violates my core style note: *"allowing the work to be the loudest thing on the page."* This image is too chaotic, the neon blues are too loud, and the sharp 3D shapes would actively compete with the text and screenshots layered on top of it. It proves that just because an AI can generate something complex, doesn't mean it belongs in a professional layout.

### The Real-Capture Note
**Why I chose real captures over AI:** 
For the *Settings Form* and *Movie Explorer* case studies, I explicitly chose to use real, cropped screenshots of the actual running application rather than using AI to generate fake, stylized dashboard mockups. While AI mockups look trendy, they are dishonest. Real captures prove actual execution, technical competence, and attention to detail. AI mockups only prove prompting ability.
